Give Every Idea a Role

Four Roles Keep the Board in Scope

Before a striking link pulls your browsing wider, give every idea one of four roles. The roles control scope — they are not styling advice, and they do not say a product is worth buying.

Anchor

The central category or purpose

The one category or use that the current browsing theme is built around.

Support

Supporting ideas for the same purpose

Ideas that answer the same goal as the anchor, without changing it.

Detail

Optional supplementary details

Extra touches you can add or drop without losing the theme.

Leave Out

Out of scope this round

Anything that does not serve the anchor — set it aside for now.

Coordination Is Not Verification

Checking Coordination Does Not Verify Anything

Re-checking whether pieces answer the same purpose is not the same as confirming a product. Alignment lines and empty frames mark what still needs confirming — they never score it.

AWhat coordination checks

  • Whether a piece answers the anchor’s purpose.
  • Whether the option and size context match what you expect.
  • Whether the color description stays consistent.

BWhat coordination cannot verify

  • Whether the item is authentic or well made.
  • Whether it will fit or look as expected.
  • Whether the color or size is truly as described.

Leave Space on the Board

When to Leave a Space Empty

If a link cannot answer the anchor’s purpose, or a detail keeps shifting, leave the space empty instead of forcing it.

  • 1

    No shared purpose. If a link does not answer the anchor’s purpose, leave it out of scope.

  • 2

    The detail keeps shifting. If size, color, or purpose changes between pages, mark it still to confirm.

  • 3

    The page cannot answer. If the external page shows no option or size context, the frame stays empty.

  • 4

    You are about to force it. If you are pushing a piece into a role it does not fit, stop and leave the space open.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an OOTDBuy spreadsheet?

An OOTDBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a reference to organize and recheck, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official OOTDBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by OOTDBuy or Google.

Does the outfit role board recommend outfits or verify products?

No. The four roles are scope-control labels, not personal styling advice. They do not generate outfits, rank trends, or verify that a product is worth buying. This site is not an outfit generator, trend board, store, QC certification, shopping agent, order tracker, or fee calculator.

What are the four roles?

Anchor, Support, Detail, and Leave Out. The anchor is the central category or purpose, Support answers the same purpose, Detail is optional, and Leave Out holds what is out of scope this round.

What should I do when a detail keeps changing?

Leave that space empty on the board. If the size, color, or purpose keeps shifting between pages, mark it as still to confirm rather than forcing an answer.

Where do I place an order?

After you have organized your roles and recorded what still needs confirming, you continue on an external page to submit the item. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
