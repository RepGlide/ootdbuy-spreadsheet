About

Last updated: September 4, 2026

What this resource is

OOTDBuy Spreadsheet is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a shared OOTDBuy spreadsheet. It links to product categories and to a fixed Google Sheet, and it explains an outfit role board — a way to organize ideas by Anchor, Support, Detail, and Leave Out so a browse stays in scope.

What this resource is not

This website is not the official OOTDBuy site, an outfit generator, a trend board, a product database, a store, a QC certification, a shopping agent, an order tracker, or a fee calculator. It does not generate color, size, or outfit scores, and it does not save user input.

Independence

This resource is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by OOTDBuy or Google. Any reference to OOTDBuy is descriptive, used to identify the spreadsheet topic this guide discusses.

No recommendation or verification

The four roles are scope-control labels, not personal styling advice. Assigning a role does not mean a product is worth buying, and checking coordination does not verify authenticity, quality, or fit.

How to use it

Open the sheet, browse a category, name one anchor, and give every other idea a role. Leave a space empty when the external page cannot answer a question, instead of forcing an answer.
