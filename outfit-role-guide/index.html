Outfit Role Guide

Anchor, Support, Detail, and Leave Out — four research roles that keep a browse in scope, without styling advice or product endorsement.

Roles control scope, not taste

When a striking link pulls your browsing wider, give the idea a role instead of following it. The four roles are labels for your own thinking — they are not personal styling advice, and they do not say a product is worth buying.

Anchor

The central category or purpose of the current browsing theme. The anchor is the one thing every other link is measured against. If nothing else on the board serves it, the board has no center.

Support

Ideas that answer the same purpose as the anchor, without changing it. A support piece stays close to the anchor’s goal and helps it hold together.

Detail

Optional supplementary touches. A detail can be added or dropped without losing the theme. It is not required for the board to make sense.

Leave Out

Anything that does not serve the anchor this round. Putting an idea here is not a judgment on the product — it is a decision to keep the current browse in scope.

A worked role assignment

  1. Anchor: the theme centers on a bag.
  2. Support: a strap idea that serves the same purpose.
  3. Detail: a charm or tag you could add or drop.
  4. Leave Out: a watch that does not serve the bag’s purpose.
